About Stummy

Stummy is an online learning platform founded in 2022 with the goal of making education accessible, enjoyable, and flexible for students. What started as a small platform offering programming tutorials has grown into a learning platform providing various IT courses, interactive exercises, and practical projects. Stummy aims to help students develop their knowledge, improve their technical skills, and build confidence through engaging learning experiences. Our mission is to empower students to learn at their own pace and prepare them for a successful future in the digital world.
